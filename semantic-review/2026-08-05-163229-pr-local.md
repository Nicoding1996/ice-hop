This change moves durable Daily, Endless, and Community completion behind legal transcript replay, immutable puzzle identities, isolated play capabilities, and user-bound issue records. It also adds ledger-backed progression, recoverable achievements, bounded issue storage, exact-body retries, and monotonic client reconciliation.

<details>
<summary>Resolved findings</summary>

- **Signed Endless phantom progression — resolved.** Completion and hint arbitration use watched transactional state, while the permanent per-tier ledger is the authoritative count. The client advances only from an accepted server result and never increments signed progression optimistically.
- **Authenticated Daily completion across UTC rollover — resolved.** Issued play evidence remains bound to the authenticated user, immutable puzzle identity, play ID, and original disclosure time, so midnight does not invalidate an active puzzle.
- **Guest Daily capability isolation and rollover — resolved.** Guest plays now receive stateless HMAC bearer capabilities backed by one Redis signing secret. Tokens bind puzzle identity, disclosure time, and a random nonce, are verified with timing-safe comparison, expire under the Daily play TTL, and do not introduce shared `anon` progression or unbounded per-guest Redis keys.
- **Guest Daily same-session recap — resolved.** Verified guest completions are remembered per puzzle for the iframe session. Registry-before-load and direct late-result reconciliation cover both response/load interleavings, including a reused active scene.
- **Legacy Community double-credit migration — resolved.** Existing played rows seed the new credit marker without incrementing creator solve totals again. New completion evidence commits candidate score, played membership, credit marker, and creator increment together.
- **Daily score/date partial writes — resolved.** Candidate score evidence is persisted before date progression. Explicit candidate provenance distinguishes fresh claims from legacy baselines: fresh candidates can repair a missing date claim, while baseline-backed legacy rows cannot create a new claim and double-count progression.
- **Community split completion state — resolved.** Score candidate, played membership, solve-credit marker, and creator solve increment commit in one watched transaction. Exact-body retries are idempotent, and signed client filtering waits for durable success.
- **Hard-at-par achievement loss — resolved.** Hard-at-par metadata commits atomically with Endless ledger evidence. Stats can derive and repair achievement state from older ledger-only records.
- **Non-monotonic signed Endless totals — resolved.** Every issue, prefetch, solve, stats, and error-recovery path merges snapshots monotonically. The final rollback branch uses `mergeProgressCount(currentValue, fromCount)`, so a stale failure cannot lower a newer confirmed total.
- **Unbounded outstanding Endless issues — resolved.** Signed issues use a capped per-user hash (`MAX_OUTSTANDING_ENDLESS_ISSUES = 16`), prune stale active records after six hours, release completed or abandoned issues, and preserve permanent ledger evidence for retries.
- **Pool drain and reservation race — resolved.** Capacity is reserved before a shared pool entry is popped or fallback generation begins. Failed or abandoned loads explicitly release reservations.
- **Prefetch races and stale writes — resolved.** Prefetch writes are generation-safe, stale responses cannot overwrite a newer puzzle, and abandoned issue IDs are released.
- **Retry after issue release — resolved.** Completion retries reconstruct their response from permanent tier-ledger evidence after the temporary issue record is gone.
- **Coverage gap — resolved.** Regression coverage now includes isolated guest Daily capabilities across midnight, puzzle-keyed guest recaps, candidate-before-date failure and repair, normal already-counted legacy Community migration, atomic Community evidence failure, Hard-at-par ledger repair, capped/released Endless issues, ledger-backed retries, stale response handling, and monotonic client progression.

<details>
<summary>Behavioral design verified</summary>

## Daily

Daily solve requests replay the exact submitted transcript against the immutable issued puzzle. Signed players retain user-bound play evidence across midnight. Guests use isolated, server-verifiable HMAC capabilities rather than a shared anonymous identity. Best-score evidence and date claims converge idempotently without letting a legacy baseline create new progression.

## Endless

The route reserves bounded issue capacity before consuming or generating a board. Hint and completion resolution are mutually exclusive, accepted completions enter a permanent per-tier ledger atomically, and temporary issues are safely released. Counts and Hard-at-par evidence are recoverable from the ledger. All client snapshots merge with the greatest confirmed total, including late prefetches and failure recovery.

## Community

A legal transcript is replayed against the exact UGC board. Best score, played state, one-time credit, and creator count are committed atomically. Legacy played rows migrate conservatively without deterministic creator-total inflation, and retries do not duplicate credit.

## Client recovery

Daily, Community, and Endless completion requests retain and retry the exact request body. Guest Daily results are puzzle-keyed for the current iframe session. Prefetch and issue lifecycle writes are generation-safe, stale/abandoned issues are released, and progression values never roll backward.
